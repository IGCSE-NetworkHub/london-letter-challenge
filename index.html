📝 Episode 1 Challenge

Listen to Episode 1 carefully, then answer the questions below.

1. How many teams are taking part in the London Letter Challenge?




2. What do the teams need to find?




3. What do they do with the letters when they find them all?




4. What does the final word or words tell the teams?




5. Who says they think they can win?




6. Who is not good with directions?




7. Who is leaving home for university?




8. Who already has a letter?




9. How does Ned feel about the challenge?




10. Why is spending time together especially important to Harry and Graham?




11. What does Clare say they need to stay?




12. What is Harry's attitude after Paula asks how he is feeling?




13. Where is the challenge taking place?




14. How many letters are hidden around London?




15. What does each team get before starting the challenge?




16. What does the clue help each team find?




17. Why is Ned probably less confident than the other teams?




18. What can we infer about Astrid and Eden?




19. Why is Graham especially excited about the challenge?




20. Which team seems the most worried about the challenge?
